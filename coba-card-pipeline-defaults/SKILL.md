# COBA card pipeline defaults

Use for any COBA investor card, brief, or tier package delivered by Front End or a sibling formatter.

## Always deliver

1. **Dense Why-Fit** — each candidate gets a tight Why-Fit that states thesis fit, Denver/Front Range tie, and the concrete ask in a few lines. No vague “aligned with wellness” filler.
2. **Person citations** — every named person is tagged with a findable source (`Person — URL` or equivalent). No bare names without a trail.
3. **Tier PDFs attached** — attach PDFs by tier every time. Do not ask “want a PDF?” and do not stop at a PNG/gallery-only package.

## Never

- Ask whether to generate a PDF when the ask was a card/package deliverable.
- Ship a gallery as the only artifact when tier PDFs are the expected handoff.
- Thin Why-Fit bullets that omit geography, check-size signal, or outreach angle when the source had them.
