# Merge Expand-In-Place

## When

- Rewriting Hunter ED1–ED8 packs with Reviewer notes (the merge bot’s job)
- After a deep-verify swarm hands domain reviews + synthesis for expand-in-place
- When the user asks for updated EDs that preserve Hunter substance

## Do

1. **Expand in place** — keep Hunter’s full detail (thesis, priority tables, candidate cards, confidence, open questions). Do not summarize away substance.
2. **Bake Reviewer notes** — correct weak/false claims, fold in material context, demote or cut failed candidates, and surface unresolved gaps inline. Never silently drop a Reviewer flag.
3. **Attach ED1–ED8 individually** — when the ask covers multiple domains, attach each ED as its own file. Do not ship only a combined package or a chat summary without the individual EDs.
4. Prefer one ED at a time unless the user asks for a full rollup — rewrite and attach one ED, then the next.

## Output

Full Hunter-detail ED files with Reviewer notes baked in; each ED attached individually (not summary-only).

## Never

- Replace the ED pack with a short executive summary
- Bundle everything into a single gallery or one mega-file when individual EDs were requested or implied
- Silently drop a Reviewer flag
